03 — Case Files

From insight to impact

Two self-initiated strategy projects, built to show how I'd approach a real brand problem — insight, then idea, then execution.

Case 01 · FMCG

Real Fruit Juice

The brand today

Real has built its name on 100% real fruit goodness — a trusted, health-focused family juice bought by parents (25–45) for kids at breakfast and at home. Strong equity, but it lives in one occasion.

The insight

Young consumers don't just buy beverages — they buy things they enjoy carrying, sharing, and drinking on the go. Real has no format built for that life: college, the gym, the commute, a picnic.

The idea

"Grab. Sip. Go."

A 150ml on-the-go bottle — easy to hold, great taste anytime, sized for a bag or a cupholder — positioning Real as the everyday healthy grab for young consumers, not just a family breakfast staple.

How I'd launch it
  • Digital: Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, creator collaborations built around real "on the go" moments.
  • Retail visibility: convenience stores, metro kiosks, office cafeterias — everywhere the occasion actually happens.
Case 02 · Retail

Decathlon

The brand today

Decathlon makes 80+ sports accessible in one destination — affordable, high quality, strong omnichannel shopping. But a store is still a store: you browse, you buy, you leave.

The insight

People trust a sport more once they've played it, not just read the spec sheet. Decathlon's biggest advantage — real courts and turf, not just aisles — is sitting unused right next to the products.

The idea

Decathlon Playground — "Try. Play. Discover."

Attached courts and turf (football, basketball, badminton, cycling, archery) where shoppers play first and buy after — turning footfall into trial, and trial into purchase.

How I'd launch it
  • The journey: discover → visit the playground → play → test equipment → purchase → become a community member.
  • Rollout: a grand-opening push, a scan-to-earn rewards program for playground visitors, and launch films of the space itself.
